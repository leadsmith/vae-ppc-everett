Common Questions

Answers to What People Ask Us Most

If you're still on the fence, these answers address the most common hesitations we hear from seniors and spouses just like you.

How much does in-home care cost in Edmonds, WA?

It depends on how many hours and what kind of help you want. Many people are surprised that starting with just a few hours a week is far more affordable than moving to a facility — and you only pay for the time you use. We give you a clear, no-surprises breakdown during your free visit. Some long-term care insurance policies and VA benefits also apply.

Will using a caregiver mean I have to leave my home?

No — it's the opposite. The whole purpose of in-home care is to help you stay in your own home, safely and comfortably, for as long as possible. We come to you so you don't have to give up your house, your routines, or your independence.

I've been caring for my spouse myself. Can you give me a break?

Yes. This is one of the most common reasons people call us. We provide respite care so you can rest, see your own doctor, run errands, or simply sleep through the night — knowing your husband or wife is with someone patient and capable. Start with a few hours; many spouses tell us it gave them their life back.

I'm not sure I want a stranger in my home. Is that normal?

Completely normal — almost everyone feels that way at first. That's why you choose your caregiver, and why many people start with just a few low-pressure hours a week. Every caregiver is background-checked, reference-verified, and trained before they ever meet you, and you can request a different match at any time, no explanation needed.

Do you help with memory loss, dementia, or Alzheimer's?

Yes. We have caregivers specifically trained in dementia and memory care — calm redirection, familiar routines, safe support, and patient, respectful communication, whether the care is for you or for your spouse.

Do I have to sign a long-term contract?

No. Care is month-to-month and completely flexible. You can add hours, reduce them, or stop entirely as your needs change. This flexibility is one of the things people tell us they value most.
